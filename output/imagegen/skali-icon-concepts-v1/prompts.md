## platform

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: exploratory iOS app icon for Skali, a self-hosted application platform for developers to deploy and manage apps, databases and servers.
Concept name: Platform.
Primary request: Create one refined, original app icon with the visual discipline, simplicity and material finish of an Apple first-party icon. Create realistic production-quality icon artwork with clear hierarchy, intentional imagery and purposeful spacing. This is icon artwork only, no UI screen.
Target dimensions: 1024 x 1024 square.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed opaque porcelain-white to very pale ice background, with a barely perceptible cool gradient.
Subject: exactly three broad, solid rounded-square architectural plates stacked vertically with small even gaps, seen in a very gentle elevated axonometric view. One compact, centered stack with a calm, unmistakable silhouette, embodying a platform supporting applications. Broad clean surfaces and thick soft edges; no server slots, no circuits, no status lights.
Style/medium: exquisitely controlled dimensional graphic illustration, translucent glacier blue and deep azure material, subtle refraction at the plate edges, soft contact shadows and quiet specular highlights. More designed graphic than photorealistic product render. Minimal geometry, balanced negative space.
Composition/framing: one symbol centered with generous safe margins, occupies approximately 62 percent of canvas width. Near-frontal, shallow perspective to keep the plates thick and readable at small home-screen sizes.
Color palette: glacier blue, azure, a hint of cyan; pale neutral background. Explore a fresh alternative to existing Skali purple.
Project grounding: current Skali uses generic black outline server on purple gradient; intentionally propose a new custom visual identity rather than restyling that glyph. Native app uses SwiftUI and targets iOS 26+.
Constraints: SINGLE icon design, square full-bleed artwork with NO rounded outer icon mask baked into the image; the OS will mask corners. No exterior mockup, no frame, no phone, no text, no labels, no letters, no watermark. No Apple logo or copied existing icon. No excessive reflections, no neon glow, no rainbow, no tiny decorative detail. Must still read as a simple bold mark in monochrome.
```

## fold

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: exploratory iOS app icon for Skali, a self-hosted application platform that helps developers deploy and manage their own apps and infrastructure.
Concept name: Fold.
Primary request: Create one original, exceptionally polished iOS icon, with the simplicity, clarity, restrained dimensionality and deliberate material finish of an Apple first-party icon. Production-quality icon graphic with strong hierarchy, balanced negative space and one memorable symbol. Icon artwork only, no UI screen.
Target dimensions: 1024 x 1024 square.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed opaque warm porcelain ivory with a very subtle soft tonal gradient.
Subject: a custom abstract capital S sculpted from one wide continuous folded ribbon. The S should have two broad horizontal sweeps joined by a short confident diagonal, open ends, a strong simple silhouette, balanced top and bottom bowls. Refined geometric construction, rounded transitions, generous open negative space. A bold proprietary monogram rather than an ordinary font character. Suggest the smooth path from development to deployment.
Style/medium: dimensional graphic illustration, a solid thick ribbon of warm vermilion and coral enamel, with restrained orange on the upper edge and darker red on side faces; minimal satin surface, precise bevels, gently rounded corners. Subtle material depth, quiet lighting and a delicate contact shadow. Primarily front-facing with only enough depth to reveal the folded construction. No twisted infinity-loop silhouette.
Composition/framing: centered custom S symbol occupying about 62 percent canvas width and 68 percent height, wide clear margins, balanced optical center, strong readability at 48 pixels.
Project grounding: the current Skali mark is a generic dark outline server on a purple gradient. This is an intentional new identity exploration; replace the generic server motif with a distinctive ownable S. The native client uses SwiftUI for iOS 26+.
Constraints: exactly ONE icon design. Full-bleed square artwork, NO rounded external icon boundary, no phone, no presentation board, no exterior frame, no words or captions; only the abstract S-shaped mark. No Apple logo, no copy of existing first-party icons. No rainbow, no neon glow, no particles, no thin outlines, no tiny detail, no excessive glass effects. The silhouette should work as a flat one-color logo too.
```

## shelter

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: exploratory iOS app icon for Skali, a self-hosted platform giving developers a stable home for their applications, databases and services on their own servers.
Concept name: Shelter.
Primary request: Design one original iOS app icon with the clarity, simplicity and polished finish of an Apple first-party icon, expressed through an architectural symbol. Production-quality icon graphic with a single subject, deliberate hierarchy, precise material finish, balanced negative space and purposeful spacing. Icon artwork only, no app interface.
Target dimensions: 1024 x 1024 square.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed opaque deep petrol blue-green, almost charcoal, with a restrained tonal gradient.
Subject: one compact abstract shelter built from two broad thick folded planes meeting at a gently peaked roof, with two short vertical supports. A simple open-front architectural pavilion, bold inverted-U silhouette with a shallow gabled top and generous empty center. No doors or windows. Suggest a protective home for apps and a reliable foundation. The proprietary symbol should feel closer to a beautiful sculptural bracket than a conventional real-estate house pictogram. Keep geometry minimal.
Style/medium: elegant dimensional graphic illustration, soft jade and sea-glass green solid material with a restrained translucent edge and crisp subtle highlight. The front is pale mint, the angled side is darker teal, gentle soft shadow behind. Controlled depth and clean geometry, no realistic building, no landscape.
Composition/framing: centered solitary symbol with generous margins, approximately 62 percent canvas width, almost straight-on with very slight perspective just enough to reveal material thickness. Strong silhouette recognizable at small size.
Project grounding: Skali's current identity is a generic dark outline server on a purple gradient. Explore a custom architectural identity instead. Native app uses SwiftUI for iOS 26+; seek calm confidence and polish.
Constraints: exactly ONE icon. Full-bleed square artwork without rounded outer corners or a baked icon frame; iOS applies the corner mask. No text, no letters, no labels, no phone, no collage, no watermark. No Apple logo or recognizable copy of Apple Home. Do not use an ordinary house outline with chimney; no chimney, no windows, no server racks, no circuitry, no status indicators, no glowing core, no particles, no rainbow. Avoid excessive neon or glass. The custom silhouette must also work as a one-color brand mark.
```
