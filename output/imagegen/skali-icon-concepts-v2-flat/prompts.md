# Skali icon concepts — flat platform refinements

Mode: built-in imagegen, using the original Platform image as an attached visual reference in each call.

Brief: keep the blue three-layer platform concept selected from the first round; reduce its rendered 3D appearance to 2D graphic shapes. No S monogram.

These are concept PNGs, not production vector masters. Original generated raster dimensions are preserved. The current app icon has not been replaced.

Displayed order for this round:

1. [Paper Stack](01-paper-stack.png) — opaque colored planes, closest to the original stack.
2. [Overlap](02-overlap.png) — transparency and intersecting shapes suggest layering.
3. [Stack Glyph](03-stack-glyph.png) — separated simple shapes on blue.

Reference: [first-round Platform](../skali-icon-concepts-v1/01-platform.png).

## paper

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: refined Skali iOS app icon concept, 1024 x 1024 square.
Direction name: Paper Stack.
Input image: reference and edit target. The user prefers its three blue stacked platforms but finds the 3D too heavy.
Primary request: redesign this exact layered-platform idea as a beautifully balanced FLAT TWO-DIMENSIONAL graphic. Keep the recognizable three-layer stack, rounded diamond geometry, centered composition, icy blue-to-azure palette and near-white background. Remove all rendered volume and physical material.
Subject: exactly three identical wide rounded rhombi, vertically staggered to make one compact stack. The upper rhombus is fully visible, the lower two are partially occluded with clean broad chevron-shaped exposed bands. Each rhombus is a single perfectly uniform opaque flat fill: light sky blue on top, vivid azure in middle, deep blue at base. Keep enough separation or contrast to read all three layers at small sizes. Refine proportions deliberately; no extra outlines.
Style: precision vector-style 2D app icon artwork with completely flat colors, generous negative space, clean shape edges and optically balanced geometry. The diagonal edges are a graphic convention, not a three-dimensional render. No visible thickness or side faces. Think layered cut-paper silhouettes viewed as a flat printed symbol, without any paper texture or shadows.
Composition: symbol centered, approximately 62 percent of square width; spacious margins.
Background: uniform opaque cool off-white.
Constraints: change the visual treatment from the reference while preserving its core stacked-platform identity. NO 3D, no extrusion, no bevels, no reflections, no highlights, no shadows, no gradients, no translucent material, no lighting, no blur, no texture. No letters, no text, no tiny details, no server hardware, no rounded outer icon mask, no border, no phone mockup, no presentation board. Full-bleed square artwork, one icon only. Original professional identity for Skali, not a copy of an existing icon.
```

## overlap

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: Skali iOS app icon concept, 1024 x 1024 square.
Direction name: Overlap.
Input image: reference and edit target for identity, blue colors and three-layer stack only.
Primary request: translate the reference's stacked blue platforms into a refined FLAT TWO-DIMENSIONAL icon using graphic transparency. Preserve the three-layer idea and icy blue family while removing the chunky 3D rendering completely.
Subject: exactly three wide rounded diamond-shaped planes, identical in shape, vertically offset and partially overlapping. The top plane is pale cyan, the middle is sky blue, bottom is azure. Each plane has a perfectly flat translucent color fill with crisp, clean boundaries. At the overlaps, flat darker blue color regions show the intersection of the shapes. The overlap pattern is the only suggestion of layering. Clearly identify three separate graphic shapes; no extruded edges or side faces. Keep the silhouette compact, balanced and memorable.
Style/medium: polished vector-style flat graphic, similar to overlapping colored acetate shapes in a printed identity, absolutely no real-world acetate material or photography. Restrained color multiplication, simple geometry, smooth rounded corners, meaningful negative space. Crisp high-quality optical balance. Keep top plane large enough to read without being washed out.
Composition/framing: centered single symbol about 62 percent canvas width, generous margins, all geometry aligned, uniform opaque very pale ice-white background.
Constraints: conceptual refinement of the provided blue platform icon; no letters or monogram. No 3D, bevel, thickness, shadows, specular highlights, blur, lighting, glow, texture or material rendering. No gradients: just uniform flat color regions and flat overlap colors. No extra circles, no nodes, no outlines, no tiny details, no text or watermark. No outer rounded icon mask, phone mockup or frame. Full square artwork, one icon only. Original distinctive Skali identity.
```

## glyph

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: exploratory Skali iOS app icon, 1024 x 1024 square.
Direction name: Stack Glyph.
Input image: reference for the three-layer platform motif, NOT for rendering style.
Primary request: reduce the reference to a compact, completely TWO-DIMENSIONAL graphic brand mark on a rich blue field. The user likes the platform stack but rejects the heavy 3D rendering and generic letter monograms. Keep the concept of three layers, simplify the shape into a clear purposeful custom glyph.
Subject: one top filled rounded rhombus, then two parallel thick downward-pointing chevrons beneath it, all in pale ice-blue and white. Together these form a single three-layer platform symbol. Use even, generous BLUE NEGATIVE-SPACE GAPS between the top rhombus and the lower chevrons; each component is clearly separate. Match the chevron diagonal angles to the lower edges of the top diamond. The two chevrons have consistent stroke-like thickness but are solid closed shapes, softly rounded joins and blunt rounded ends. Highly balanced and meticulously spaced. Bold simple silhouette, without thin outlines.
Composition: perfectly centered compact symbol occupying approximately 56 percent of canvas width, considerable empty blue space around it, balanced optical centering.
Color palette: uniform saturated azure-blue full-bleed square background. Top diamond solid white, middle chevron very pale ice blue, bottom chevron light sky blue. Graphic contrast, not lighting.
Style/medium: immaculate flat vector-style identity, deliberate optical geometry, smooth clean edges and flat uniform fills. Feels refined and appropriate beside native Apple icons through disciplined proportion and simplicity, not copied from any Apple icon.
Constraints: no 3D whatsoever, no bevel, no side faces, no thickness in space, no shadows, no gradient, no gloss, no glass, no glow, no highlights, no texture. No letters, no S, no server hardware, no nodes, no detail, no wordmark, no watermark. Do not bake rounded outside corners into the square artwork. No phone mockup, no frame, no collage. ONE original Skali app icon only.
```
